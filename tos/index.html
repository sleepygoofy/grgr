1. About the Bot

REDLINE Bot ("the Bot") is a companion Discord application for the REDLINE browser-based racing game hosted at redline.lat. It surfaces in-game information (player stats, leaderboards, crews, market data, live auctions) inside Discord servers and lets authorized staff broadcast announcements to the game from Discord.

By adding the Bot to a Discord server, or by interacting with the Bot's commands, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, do not invite the Bot or use its commands.

2. Who Can Use It

You may use the Bot if you meet all of the following:

  • You are at least 13 years old, which is also Discord's own minimum age requirement.
  • You agree to comply with the Discord Terms of Service and Discord Community Guidelines.
  • You are not banned from the REDLINE platform.

Server administrators are responsible for ensuring their server complies with these terms and Discord's policies.

3. Acceptable Use

You agree not to use the Bot to:

  • Spam, flood, or otherwise abuse Bot commands
  • Attempt to bypass rate limits, authentication, or staff-only commands
  • Reverse-engineer, exploit, or interfere with the Bot's operation
  • Harass other users, post slurs, or share content that violates Discord's Community Guidelines
  • Use the Bot to facilitate cheating, account-sharing, real-money trading of in-game items, or any other behavior prohibited by the REDLINE game's own rules
  • Impersonate REDLINE staff or claim affiliation with the project without authorization

We may rate-limit, block, or remove the Bot from any server at our discretion for violations.

4. Data We Collect

The Bot collects only what it needs to function:

  • Discord User ID — used to link your Discord identity to a REDLINE game account when you run /stats or similar commands
  • Discord Username — displayed in command responses and broadcast logs
  • Server (Guild) ID — used to scope server-specific settings and to track where the Bot is installed
  • Command Input — the text of commands you send to the Bot, used solely to fulfill the request

The Bot does not read or store the content of regular messages in your server. The Bot does not use the Discord Message Content intent unless explicitly disclosed in a future update.

The Bot does not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. How Data Is Stored

Linked account data and persistent settings are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), the same backend the REDLINE game uses. Access is restricted to project owners and authorized staff via Firebase security rules.

Note: messages broadcast from Discord into the in-game feed are recorded with the sender's Discord username so players can see who sent them. Don't broadcast anything you wouldn't want associated with your Discord identity.

Deletion requests are honored within 30 days. Some operational logs may be retained briefly for fraud prevention and abuse handling, then purged.
